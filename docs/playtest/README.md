# Trap Dash Arena — Anonymized Playtest Records, Observation Sheets & Phone Test Matrix

## 1. Data Privacy & Ethics Compliance (RA 10173)
In strict compliance with the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)** and ITSD 85 Course Guidelines:
* No personal identifying information (real names, student numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, faces, or voice recordings) was collected or stored in this repository.
* All participants provided informed consent prior to the 15–20 minute session and are referenced exclusively by anonymized codes (`T1` through `T7`).
* Initial evaluation was conducted with $n = 5$ external participants (`T1`–`T5`), followed by a verification retest with $n = 2$ additional external participants (`T6`–`T7`) after implementing three playtest-driven fixes (total $N = 7$).

## 5. Action Plan & Retest Verification (Table IV Data)

| Issue Found | Testers Reporting | Severity | Fix Implemented in Codebase | Fixed? | Result After Retest ($n = 2$: T6, T7) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Invisible control inversion in Level 3** caused disorientation and repeated pit deaths when crossing `ControlInverterZone`. | 3 of 5 (`T2`, `T4`, `T5`) | **Major** | Added an overhead bidirectional warning arrow (`<->`) above `Player` whenever `controlsInverted` is active, plus red hazard outlines in `High-Contrast Trap Tells` mode (`lib/game/actors/player.dart`, `control_inverter_zone.dart`). | Yes | Level 3 completion rose from 80% (4/5) to **100% (2/2)**; average attempts dropped from **3.60 to 2.00**; Statement 4 rose from 4.00 to 4.50. |
| **2. Manual IPv4 entry in Online Lobby** led to typos and slow connection setup on mobile keyboards (Statement 8 $M = 3.40 < 3.50$). | 3 of 5 (`T2`, `T4`, `T5`) | **Major** | Implemented UDP broadcast LAN host discovery (`NetManager.discoverHosts` on port `7778`, `FIND HOSTS` quick-join button) and a one-tap IP clipboard copy button in `LobbyScreen` (`lib/autoload/net_manager.dart`, `lib/ui/lobby_screen.dart`). | Yes | Statement 8 (*"Joining an online match was easy"*) increased from **$M = 3.40$ to $M = 4.50$** on retest; join setup time fell from ~28s to <5s. |
| **3. Lack of opponent spatial awareness in 1v1 Online Mode** when only viewing text scores in the top HUD bar. | 2 of 5 (`T1`, `T3`) | **Minor** | Added `RivalGhost` (`lib/game/actors/rival_ghost.dart`), rendering a 45%-opacity translucent silhouette of the opponent with a floating nickname badge interpolated at 0.50s intervals alongside live coin/score HUD counters. | Yes | Statement 9 (*"The online match felt responsive"*) rose from **$M = 4.40$ to $M = 5.00$** on retest; both retest players praised the live ghost visual. |
